The brief

What they needed

The problem

Plenty of utes, vans and trucks sit parked on their days off, while people with a job on can’t easily find the right size truck, or tell whether their licence even covers it.

The goal

One marketplace for both sides: renters find a truck their licence allows, see the full price and book in minutes, and owners approve every rental and earn from idle days.

What we built

The work

01

Licence first

Tap your licence class and see only the trucks you can legally drive, matched by GVM. A licence guide explains every class, from a car licence to multi-combination.

02

Not sure what size?

Pick what you’re moving, from a studio apartment to pallets of stock or a load of soil, and it suggests a truck size and shows the matches.

03

Checks built into checkout

Six steps: trip, driver, checks, cover, payment, done. Kilometres, cover, fees and the bond are all on screen before anyone pays.

04

Owners stay in control

Owners see what an idle truck could earn, approve every rental, block out their own days and get paid after each job.
