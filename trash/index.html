The brief

What they needed

The problem

Getting rid of one big item usually means sending photos, guessing cubic metres and waiting on a call back for a quote.

The goal

Let people pick the exact item, see an all-in price straight away and book online, with prices that are always right.

What we built

The work

01

Every item has a price

61 items in 11 categories, from mattresses to pianos, each with an all-in price and no call-out fee. The total updates as you add.

02

Postcode first

The first step checks the collection postcode against the real service area: 417 postcodes within 150 km of Geelong. Mixed loads and clean-outs get handed to JUNK.

03

A booking, not a quote

Items go into a running cart, then three quick questions on stairs, urgency and dismantling, all priced up front.

04

Prices that can’t drift

The supplied price list lives with the code. Tests check that the browser, the server and that list all agree, so a typo fails a test instead of reaching a customer.
