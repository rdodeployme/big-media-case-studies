The brief

What they needed

The problem

Old road signs, hospital beds, X-ray machines, racking: if it’s mostly metal, this service collects it. But customers didn’t know what counted, what it would cost, or where it ended up.

The goal

Make booking as easy as calling a tradie, price it before the truck comes, and prove where the metal went afterwards.

What we built

The work

01

Book, quote, collect, recycle, report

A five-step flow that tells customers exactly what happens and when, from a two-minute online booking to a statement at the end.

02

What we collect, at a glance

A visual index of everything the service takes, from guardrail and bollards to whitegoods and medical equipment, so nobody has to ask.

03

Sector pages that speak the language

Separate pages for councils and state roads, aged care, hospitals, universities, and business and government, each with its own examples.

04

A portal for the paperwork

Four numbers for every collection, and a client portal where customers see their own statements.
