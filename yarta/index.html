The brief

What they needed

The problem

Yarta turns scattered environmental data into one score, a report a council can hand over, and a roadmap to improve. It’s a technical product sold to council officers and business owners, who don’t have time to decode a method.

The goal

A site that explains the method in plain English, and a demo good enough that buyers have already tried the product before a sales call.

What we built

The work

01

A demo you can click

A live portal with sample data for eight sectors: council, business, construction, education, health, logistics, manufacturing and retail. Buyers see their own kind of organisation, not a generic chart.

02

The report, before they buy

A full sample Yarta Report shows exactly what a council receives: the score, the benchmarks behind it, and the ranked roadmap.

03

Lead qualification built in

A five-minute Readiness Check gives prospects a first read on where they stand and tells the sales team who is ready to talk.

04

Pricing without the phone call

Simple, published pricing by organisation size, with the first-year offer up front, so councils can budget before they ever enquire.
