The brief

What they needed

The problem

Recycle Group runs transfer stations, recycling lines, trucks and heavy plant. Safety checks were only worth anything if they happened at the start of a shift, beside the machine, on whatever phone was in someone’s pocket.

The goal

Rather than pay for off-the-shelf safety software and bend the process to fit, build a system around the group’s own machines, sites and policies.

What we built

The work

01

Daily checks in seconds

About 58 check types across eight groups, from fit-for-work declarations to vehicles, machinery, site checks and high-risk work. Each one is a tap from the home screen.

02

Faults that get closed

Report a fault, and it goes straight to a manager follow-up with the action taken, whether more is needed, and a signature. Nothing sits in a drawer.

03

Training for each machine

Step-by-step modules for specific plant, like the alligator shear, canister puncturer and scrap metal baler, with hazards, controls and sign-off.

04

Policies, acknowledged

62 policies across five departments, read and acknowledged on the phone, with the record kept against each worker.
